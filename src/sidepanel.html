Nano A11y Audit

Getting Started

Upload a CSV of URLs for the pages in your test sample to begin audit. The extension will visit each page in your sample, and will run checks with axe-core and Gemini Nano. It will then download the report data, and open the WCAG EM Report Tool to view the results.

For guidance on creating a test sample, please review the W3C Conformance Evaluation Methodology Step 2: Explore the Website and Step 3: Select a Representative Sample
