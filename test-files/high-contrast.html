High Contrast (Forced Colors) Failures

Fail: CSS Background Icon

This button uses `background-image` for its icon. In High Contrast Mode, the image is stripped, leaving an empty box.

Fail: Shadow Border

This button relies on `box-shadow` for its border. In High Contrast Mode, the shadow is stripped, and the blue background turns black, making the button look like plain text or invisible.

This button uses a real border and an `img` tag. Both survive High Contrast Mode.
